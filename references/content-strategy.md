# Content Strategy

Use this reference after source preflight and before NotebookLM generation. The goal is to turn reliable source material into a package that is worth publishing, not merely a summary.

## Package Modes

- `standard_article`: one source, one short video, one infographic or cover, platform copy.
- `long_report`: original source plus optional source-derived briefing companion, short-video hook, deep-summary post, long-form notes, and visual/carousel opportunities.
- `news_update`: fast hook, what changed, why it matters, what to watch next.
- `tutorial`: before/after workflow, steps, mistakes, checklist.
- `case_study`: how it worked, numbers, replicable pattern, failure risks.

## Angle Selection

Pick 1 primary angle and 1 backup angle before generating media.

Strong primary angles:

1. A clear tension: what changed, broke, accelerated, or became newly possible?
2. A surprising detail: a number, quote, behavior, or case that makes people lean in.
3. A useful decision lens: what should a manager, creator, founder, or operator do differently?
4. A visual story: a framework, comparison, timeline, or checklist that can become a cover or carousel.

Avoid weak angles:

- "This article is important."
- "Here are many points."
- "AI is changing everything."
- Tool-first hooks unless the source is actually about the tool.

## Bilingual Handling

Chinese copy can be more direct and platform-native. English copy should explain local context when needed and avoid untranslated brand assumptions.

Do not translate the Chinese caption mechanically. Re-select the hook for the English audience.

## Self-Media Operating Loop

For each package, create:

1. Short-video hook for immediate posting.
2. Deep-summary post for users who want substance.
3. Visual asset or carousel candidate.
4. Repost angle for the next day, such as a checklist, quote card, or "what this means" follow-up.

Track after publishing:

- title/hook used
- platform
- asset filenames
- publish time
- observed comments or follow-up questions
- next content angle

## Quality Gate

Before finalizing:

1. The hook names the actual content topic.
2. The source archive exists and was not truncated.
3. The primary angle is traceable to the source.
4. Chinese and English outputs are audience-specific.
5. Upload filenames in publish files match disk files.
6. The execution note records NotebookLM source count and any blockers.
